## Handoff

### What changed

`rule-based-v0` replaces the wait stub in `agents/rule_based/` (`policy.py`, `keys.py`;
`stub.py` removed; `RuleBasedPolicy` is still exported from `__init__.py`). It implements
Rule Policy §1-§7: R0 working-state update, R1-R12, §2.1 seeded tie-break, §4 commitment,
exploration and cue filter, §5 back-off keyed by (action kind, target), §6 canteen and the
fill probe, replies, S4/S5 switches, §7.3 transaction and §7.4 decision-only cue capture.
The policy imports only `world.protocol`, `agents.types`, `agents.policies._plans` and the
standard library. Fixtures `s1-spring-visible/orig.toml` (commit `d533cd7`) and
`s3-regrow288/orig.toml` (commit `b5f512f`) were each committed once and never touched again.
No spec, ADR, frozen type, world, sim or persistence file changed. No OQ was added.

Tests (`backend/tests/unit/`): `test_rule_policy_vectors.py` (DV-01 to DV-09, DV-12 to DV-15,
exact plans and states, DV-02a/b, DV-04 to DV-06 probe cases), `test_rule_policy_scenarios.py`
(DV-10, DV-11a/b in the engine; fill probe outcomes empty/partial/full/capacity 0, drink and
give in the engine; invalid plan refused by the world with §5 back-off; no-repeat checker with
a planted negative case and an engine run; S4/S5 on/off comparisons; OQ-074),
`test_rule_policy_contract.py` (import and forbidden-field leak tests, hypothesis determinism
property with save/restore, restore-validator check on every committed state, fixture diffs
and map hash, S1 spring visible at tick 0). `backend/tests/dev_runs/test_rule_policy_runs.py`
holds the long checks, skipped unless `P2_RULE_RUNS=1` (outside the default suite, OQ-053).
