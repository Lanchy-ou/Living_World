## Handoff

### What changed

The resident coordinator starts selection after logging the confirming delivery and its mechanical writes. It freezes that delivery's prefix, entries, current state, pending membership and store revision. Subsequent deliveries continue into the live period. A living K delivery returns `waiting` while a call is outstanding. The host retains the packet and offers it again when the workflow is ready. World death is a separate notification because the engine delivers no living packet for the deceased.

At K, the coordinator applies the validated change set to the latest mechanical fields, binds new IDs after intervening allocations, archives the period, settles pending membership and writes the K packet into the next period. Memory-off uses the same boundary and resets the current intention. Early wake keeps the working context. A failed call retains the complete closed period, including entries received after confirmation. Only periods sent in full on a committed change-set request leave pending.

Both stages use DL-01's framed guard, fake-tested attempt port and write-only journal. Prepared requests, dispatches and terminals have durable cursors. The coordinator emits packet membership, revision and consolidation receipts, hashes and a serializable resident checkpoint. A restored worker can consume the persistence owner's verified dispatch and terminal. A missing terminal produces the interrupted-call disposition. A sealed worker leaves late replies to inspection. DL-04 owns the shared runner wiring and DL-05 owns durable save/replay integration under M14 §3.
