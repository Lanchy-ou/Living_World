Bind inferred sources to the immutable sent or already staged revision and observed sources to a delivered subject witness. Index-only IDs can support an inference. Mutating an existing record requires a full sent or authorized staged record.
- Pre-scan all explicit operands/producers. Stage operations in listed order, with the §3.5 first-failure precedence, declared/prospective writes and transitive dependencies. Prevent cycles, reserve later mutation targets, roll back rejected ops including backlinks, flags and capacity victims, and apply independent valid ops. Return per-op verdicts, deterministic evictions and pinned revision bindings for commit.
- Enforce sourced trait drift, immutable skill hypotheses, mechanical fields and kind-specific capacities. Carry speech ancestry from the current staged revision and newly bound direct source revisions, including across eviction and restore. Derive possible_relabel flags against the committed store, as sorted inspection-only sidecars. Preserve enough input and output for MF-03 to rebase on later mechanical writes and for EV-01 to export the flags.

- Own **MM-06, MM-07, MM-08, MM-09, MM-10, MM-11, MM-12, MM-20, MM-22, MM-25** and **MF-06, MF-07, MF-09, MF-19, MF-24**. Cover every case in those frozen rows, including the listed trust defaults, anti-relabelling and positive controls.
- Exercise every authority-table cell and first-failure reason, dependencies on a dropped producer, a later protected capacity victim, duplicate/cyclic refs, a guessed or guard-omitted operand, and a legitimate inference from an index entry. Verify independent writes survive and no side effect of a rejected operation leaks. Retain a wrong but well-sourced belief with the world-state reader set to fail on access.
- Exercise archived/superseded/refuted records and eviction on a fixture store, with deterministic tie-breaks. Check no forbidden witness can turn speech about a spring into direct observation.
