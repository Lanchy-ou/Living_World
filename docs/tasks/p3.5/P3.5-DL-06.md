Show one schema on the wire and no private reasoning in the next input. Missing required wire support, changed model mapping or insufficient budget blocks dispatch.

Unexpected output, a retry or a failed dispatch stays visible in the denominator and ledger. An incomplete or failed check withholds step 2. - The cost/day forecast uses the live rates and all sample costs, including failed/paid attempts, with EV-05's per-kind calls/day and growing contexts. A missing usable measurement blocks the estimate or uses a documented conservative bound. Report actual measured figures separately from reservations.
