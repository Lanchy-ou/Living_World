### Goal and non-goals

P3.5-EN-08 cut 24.8% of the time per tick. A reviewer profile of the
121-tick prefix on the EN-08 revision (54 s under cProfile) still puts about
80% of run time in `deepcopy`:

- `sim/v2/runner.py` `Runner.advance`, about 14 s. `World.pending_packets`
  already returns detached packets. The runner copies each packet again for
  the delivery callback and again for the decision callback, and it
  deep-copies each submission.
- `world/v2/engine/tick.py` `World.step`, about 12 s. `step` deep-copies the
  whole `WorldState` as its transaction. That includes `static_map`, the rule
  `library` and other values that no tick changes.
- The frozen v1 `agents/runtime.py`, about 17 s. This is out of scope: v1
  modules are frozen.

Goal: remove copies that protect nothing, and keep every isolation that the
specs and approved rulings require. These are the detached packet delivery
of OQ-P3.5-PR-05, the atomic tick (a refused boundary leaves the world
unchanged), and the isolation of committed choices from later callback
edits. A copy that remains needs a reason in a comment.

Non-goals: changes to v1 modules, to the protocol models, or to settlement
semantics.

### What changed

- The engine writes committed boundary packets. The journal serializes its own detached batch, and the runner keeps a separate detached batch for `turn_reasons`, `check_binding`, and retries. The receiver now gets its own batch directly from `World.pending_packets`; the runner no longer deep-copies its held packets for that callback.
- The receiver and decision callback can edit their respective packets. Both now use the same detachment logic as `World.pending_packets`. Each consumer owns its tile lists, while the frozen tile rows and coordinates are shared. Other mutable descendants, including reports, entity lists, and feedback, are still deep-copied. The runner still owns copies of callback submissions and terminal witnesses for settlement, refusal records, and replay.
- Initialization and restore supply the map, clock, senses, dialogue limits, and cues. Tick phases read them. Rule preparation supplies installed definitions, which the schedule and settlement read. The staged tick shares these inputs while retaining a private library index and hash fields for rule additions. Acknowledgement removes entries from a private pending-delivery map. Fatigue and report construction only read the opening packets before perception builds new packets. The transaction deep-copies the remaining state and publishes it after settlement.
- The focused callback regression mutates visible and explored tile lists and nested observable signs. The decision callback still receives the original packet, and the engine and journal keep their original bytes. The existing PR-05, PR-06, and EN-08 tests exercise scheduling, binding, refusals, retries, and edits to callback-owned submissions.
