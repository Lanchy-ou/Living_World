## Handoff

### What changed

Implemented the two world-level utterance checks and emission in the existing
completion schedule. Speech preserves start failures, counts addressed turns
against the pair's quiet gap and cooldown, and publishes the speaker's feedback
and listeners' heard utterances through the tick context.

Proposals retain the exposed references and parties in their terms. The first
valid reply marks the proposal answered. Acceptance registers an agreement and
records backward causal links to both utterances. Speech and agreement IDs
come from the objective event cursor.

Added 56 focused tests for term and reply validation, the two actor orders,
continue and attachment timing, session limits, hearing, and event causality.

Gate 2 B1 moves the companion intent ID and payload self-ID into
`EventWriter.emit` so the complete event is logged in one operation. The
regression test captures the logged utterance before control returns to the
speech helper and verifies its association with the completed action.
