### What changed

- `Runtime.decide` gives `_LegacyPlanner` a detached request. The adapter now shares the private perception with the frozen rule-based policy and still copies the feedback it injects from the host checkpoint.
- `PolicyHost.model_packet` now returns the `ModelBoundary` produced by typed construction. Its protocol models rebuild mutable lists from the runner's packet before handing the view to a model or memory consumer.
- `World.pending_packets` now copies the visible and explored tile lists while sharing their frozen rows and coordinates. The engine builds the committed packets, and the packet journal and coordinator read through the detached accessor. The rest of each packet is deep-copied, including nested entity and feedback lists.
- The two focused regressions mutate data returned by the world and by the model-facing accessor. They verify stable source packets, independent readers, and the journal. The existing PR-05 retry and committed-choice controls remain part of full-suite CI.
