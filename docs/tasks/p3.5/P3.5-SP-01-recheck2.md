## Blocking

### R4-B01 — The validator loses speech ancestry when an ancestor is forgotten

**Location.** MM §§4.1–4.2, 5–6 and 8, MM-06. MF §3.5. Decision sheet
§3, revised MM-D5.

**Conflict.** A record takes the speech-derived cap through the exact
revision cited by an `inferred` source. MM §4.1 preserves revision bindings
for inspection, including evicted revisions. MM §§5–6 remove forgotten
records from the live store, and MF §3.5 disallows reading audit from
validation. The live store and checkpoint contracts do not require the
speech classification of a cited revision to survive separately from that
revision. An ID, version and hash identify a source without revealing its
classification.

**Counterexample.** B keeps a told belief T, then derives I with an
`inferred` source pointing to T's revision. T is forgotten and the save is
restored. B proposes a new belief at confidence 4 with a valid direct
observation and an inferred source pointing to I. I is available in the
sent input. Option B requires rejection because T was speech-derived, but
the validator cannot read T. With an otherwise equivalent independently
observed T, the proposed confidence can be legal. Treating all unavailable
ancestors alike cannot satisfy both cases.

**Minimum correction.** Persist a code-owned speech-ancestry summary keyed
to the immutable revision or its bound source. Make it available to the
validator and replay after source eviction, with no access to forgotten
content or the audit. Compute it from the candidate revision on every
source-changing operation, including adding speech to a confidence-4
record. Test direct speech, merge and transitive inference before and
after eviction and restore, with an independent-source control.

### R4-B02 — The inspection artifact cannot carry the required flag and link

**Location.** MM §4.2, MF §3.5 and MF-25, EV §§1–2. Decision sheet §3,
revised MM-D5.

**Conflict.** MF directs code to mark `possible_relabel` after commit, and
EV §2 requires the memory browser to show the marked record and the
overlapping told record from its store shards. EV §1 defines a closed
`StoreInspection` shape with records and index lines. Neither it nor the
MM §2 record envelope has the inspection flag or its link. The model's
index is meant to omit the diagnostic. The frontend has no authorized
source for the required view.

**Counterexample.** MF-25's observed-only spring belief is accepted beside
the live told belief and must show a flag with that belief's identity.
Exporting only the specified store-shard fields loses the flag and link.
Adding them to the shared memory record or index would require a new
contract and could put the diagnostic in the resident's projection.

**Minimum correction.** Define a typed inspection-only sidecar in the
store shard or an indexed audit record. Retain the flagged record and
matching told revision IDs captured at evaluation of the flag. Test the
export and viewer against MF-25, including that the serialized resident
projection contains neither the flag nor its diagnostic link.

## Non-blocking

### R4-N01 — MF-24 expects the wrong first failure reason

**Location.** MF §3.5 and MF-24.

MF-24 submits a new belief at confidence 4 with both a valid told source
and an unrelated hunger citation, and expects `provenance`. The new cap
makes it speech-derived at 4, and §3.5 checks `format` before
`provenance`. The rejection is determinate and the test's expected
diagnostic needs alignment. Use confidence 3 for the hunger witness
negative, with an otherwise valid control. Exercise the confidence-4
`format` rejection separately using the valid spring sighting in MF-25.
