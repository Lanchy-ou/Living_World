### Goal and scope

Give the operator a reproducible answer to how much of CNY 20 remains before each paid dispatch. The ledger covers every P3.5 provider charge. Keep P3 smoke balances separate.

**Allowed files and forbidden dependencies.** Write `backend/src/living_island/analysis/v2_spend.py`, `backend/tests/v2/spend_ledger/**` and `docs/tasks/p3.5/P3.5-spend-ledger.md`. Read sanitized call artifacts and price evidence. No provider access, account mutation, credentials, simulation dependencies or live IO is needed. Preserve concurrent branch isolation by keeping this module outside the future `analysis/v2/` initializer. The card may update its own handoff. The shared-file allocation and M13 exclusions in [M14 plan §3](M14-plan.md#3-file-ownership) apply.

### Deliverables

- A cross-platform offline ledger projection with a documented invocation and a human-readable ledger. Record date, run and stage, model/alias and verified version, logical calls and dispatched attempts, unique attempt IDs or content-addressed references, actual input (cache hit/miss), output tokens, dated CNY price source, settled CNY, outstanding conservative reserve, phase total and remaining headroom. Show provider-reported charges/invoice differences when available. Keep full prompts and keys out of the ledger.
- Capture opening P3.5 spend with evidence or mark it unresolved. Group rows by paid check and resident run. Those later cards regenerate their rows using this tool, one writer at a time. Fake fixtures are labelled and stay out of paid totals.
- Supply a cost sizing sheet by call kind, day and attempt for the operator. Reconcile complete and partial records after failures, interruptions and restore.

### Acceptance

- From a fixture with success, one retried request, abandoned call with late usage and a never-dispatched floor, reproduce the counted charges by hand. An API error with a possible charge stays reserved until resolved. A missing usage or cache split is explicitly unknown. Conservatively hold the pinned upper bound, including output and full-price uncached input.
- Count each dispatched attempt once across resume, including retries, failed, interrupted or abandoned work. Release a reservation on recorded settlement. Identical input hashes rendered for different paid attempts are distinct expenses. A `stopped_unfit` record without dispatch costs zero. Re-running on the same records produces identical rows and totals.
- Use exact monetary units and dated peak prices. Subtract settled P3.5 charges and all unresolved reservations from CNY 20. A planned dispatch or concurrent in-flight exposure that cannot fit is withheld outside the simulation. An unknown opening balance or unbounded liability blocks paid work. No cost-based gameplay rule or automatic fallback is introduced.
