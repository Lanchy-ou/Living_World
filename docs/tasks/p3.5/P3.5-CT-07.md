### Acceptance (positive, negative, edge, determinism)

- **CT-05.** Knapping consumes a held nodule stack of exactly one and yields two flakes. Twist three grass fibres, create the axe, cut deadwood and show the rate comparison. Light a fire, shape clay, insert the unfired pot, bake it, fill it and drink from it. Mark a grass site, deliver its frame/cover/tie and complete the shelter with other residents. Record the 24 work units, contributor IDs and single process closure/output. Compare shelter sleep with cave and ordinary sleep at unclamped stamina.
- **CT-06.** Actor and watcher see vine strength as “strong” and grass as “weak.” For a vine-cord axe attempt the visible start shows head, handle and cord. Settle shows `snaps`, the cord gone, and returned head and handle with matching archetypes/materials and newly allocated IDs. Isolate returned items from equal stacks to observe their new IDs. Grass cord holds. With frame and cover present, vine cord alone yields zero site work. Replace it with grass cord and show multiple near residents contributing at one unit each per tick. Record visible progress as well as objective units. No hidden properties or rule IDs leak into watcher packets.
- **CT-16 (pot).** A held empty pot next to the spring ends at exactly one serving. A second fill gives `already_full`, with no extra water. Drinking consumes the serving. Keep evidence of the separate inherited canteen. Retain the same canonical trace after a mid-craft resume.
- Show §5.9 through actual perceived use, timings and outcomes. Live discovery, memory and learning claims go to M15.
