## Handoff

### What changed

- Built one pending packet per living resident at tick zero and after each tick.
  Packets include final sight, last intermediate sightings, best cue detail,
  explored static terrain, inventory, running action, speech and action feedback.
  Acknowledgement consumes the old packet and supplies the previous need bands
  and fatigue counts. Tick zero seeds exposure receipts silently.
- Captured action phases as they happen and selected watchers after physiology
  and waking. Apparent start looks survive consumption and actor death.
  Delivery filters targets against the watcher's own sight or inventory and
  exposes only watcher-visible phenomena. A successful give witnesses the exact
  moved portions for its observing recipient, including split-then-merge.
- The packet tests cover WAP-05, WAP-07, WAP-08, WAP-14, WAP-17, WAP-19 and
  WAP-31/32/36/37, EN-25/26/32, RR-13, and both Round 3 paths. The EN-06
  follow-up verifies addressed hearing, dead residents and awake residents
  outside hearing range through real packets. The mixed dawn packet asserts
  the complete ordered report array through progress. The wake-boundary gift
  asserts a second exact array ending in `woke`.
- The packet tests use a synthetic test-pack analogue for sight while asleep.
  Packet restore and legacy trigger replay belong to EN-07 and the v1 adapter
  cards. Gift memory accounting belongs to M14.
