# P3.5-SP-01: Owner decision sheet

Every product choice that ADRs 0008–0014 left open, with the answer each draft
spec applies. The owner answered on 2026-11-18 (§3): two decisions changed
(EV-D1, EV-D7) and the rest stand as recommended. The drafts apply those
answers, and the Gate 2 round 1 fix plan the owner approved the same day.
§4 lists the rework choices the owner confirms at the freeze. A later change
to any of them revises every spec it touches.

The decisions are listed per spec, in the order of the spec's own decision
table. §1 picks out the ones with the largest effect on what the residents and
the owner will see.

## 2. All decisions

### 2.1 Rule representation (`docs/spec/rule-representation-v0.1.md`)

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| RR-D1 | Does a rule's success chance depend on the inputs or on skill? | Fixed per rule. Better inputs can get a better chance through a more specific rule. No world-side skill | Simple and testable. A world-side skill would carry knowledge across waking periods in the memory-off arm |
| RR-D2 | How is matter measured? | Units per matter class (`mineral`, `plant`, `water`) per archetype | Mass values need balancing of their own; a unit count alone cannot tell stone from wood |
| RR-D3 | What does an output inherit? | The input's material with its true and apparent values; archetype properties win; instance overrides are not inherited | Flint and stone axes then differ by material, as ADR 0008 D3 intends, and deception stays per material |
| RR-D4 | The first vocabulary | 5 needs, 3 matter classes, 17 properties, 6 compatibility verbs plus 5 starter verbs (`combine`, `strike`, `cut`, `light`, `build`), 31 phenomena | Small enough to test, enough for the starter content of ADR 0010 D2 |
| RR-D5 | How are phenomena of unmatched attempts made? | Per-verb comparison tables over true values; the union of holding entries, else a default | Gives physical feel without naming a rule (ADR 0007 D5) |
| RR-D6 | Do stackable items record an owner? | No; only non-stackable entities do | Merging stacks would mix owners; `item_transferred` events keep who gave what |

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

### 2.4 Memory model (`docs/spec/memory-model-v0.1.md`)

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| MM-D1 | Capacity of each kind | Episodic 300, belief 200, skill 80, social one per known resident, commitment unlimited, intention 1; history bounded per kind. At capacity, episodic adds are dropped, beliefs give way lowest confidence and least recently used first, skills give way refuted first (ADR 0011 D1) | A week fills less than half; the index stays readable for consolidation |
| MM-D2 | Personality traits and drift | Five traits on 0–4 (curiosity, caution, sociability, trustfulness, diligence), drift one step from base | Few enough to read in the prefix, enough to tell the three profiles apart |
| MM-D3 | Trust to default confidence of told claims | Trust 0 → 0, 1–2 → 1, 3 → 2, 4 → 3 | Hearsay starts below what one saw; full trust still falls short of certainty |
| MM-D4 | Is a commitment to oneself a commitment? | No, it is an intention; commitments are accepted agreements only | Every commitment can then be judged from objective events |
| MM-D5 | Confidence cap for hearsay | A record with no observed or innate source never exceeds 3 | A resident may believe a liar fully, but never be certain without seeing |

### 2.5 Memory formation (`docs/spec/memory-formation-v0.1.md`)

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| MF-D1 | Do hard facts reach a party who did not perceive them? | Yes: `sim` feeds each party the agreement and give facts it is part of, to the memory writer only | Owner PR-D5 gives both parties a record; the facts are about the resident itself and never enter its context directly |
| MF-D2 | K | 36 ticks (6 hours) | A normal night consolidates; a nap or an interrupted sleep does not; equals the faint length |
| MF-D3 | When do consolidation calls start? | At the start of a sleep chosen at K or longer, or of a faint; the result is committed at `s + K` | The world keeps running while the model works; a nap costs no call |
| MF-D4 | The change-set schema | Nine closed ops: add, update, merge, supersede, confidence, review_done, forget, commitment_status, trait | Covers every change ADR 0012 D2 names, and nothing that touches hard facts |
| MF-D5 | Consolidation inputs that do not fit | Leave out extra selected records, then pending periods, then the oldest day-log entries; left-out entries are not consolidated; mandatory parts are never left out | Answers ADR 0012 Q8 without a model summary |
| MF-D6 | Retries and the pending limit | 3 retries per call, 1 parse retry, at most 2 pending periods | Bounded, and a failing provider cannot pile up unread days |
| MF-D7 | Selection limit | At most 60 records read per consolidation, beyond the mandatory ones | Bounds the call while leaving the model a real choice |

### 2.6 Decision loop (`docs/spec/decision-loop-v0.1.md`)

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

### 2.7 Evaluation (`docs/spec/evaluation-v0.1.md`)

| ID | Question | Recommended answer, applied in the draft | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| EV-D1 | What counts as a hop of information | A heard utterance retold at a turn whose context held it and whose output cites it; uncited retellings reported apart. Each hop counted for acceptance is also checked by hand: the retelling must carry the earlier message, garbled or embellished included (owner, 2026-11-18) | Every counted hop is then traceable, as plan §4.1 requires; a citation alone may point at something else |
| EV-D2 | How skills are scored | Code-counted evidence: expected phenomena seen ÷ applications | Matches ADR 0014 D2; deception cannot make a correct lesson look wrong |
| EV-D3 | When a `be_at` promise is kept | At the place within 6 ticks (one hour) either side of `due_tick` | Residents cannot time a tick exactly; one hour is a fair meeting window |
| EV-D4 | Time-use classes | Survival, crafting, exploring, social, idle; sleep apart; talking as an overlay | Covers ADR 0014 D1's four shares; speech rides alongside actions |
| EV-D5 | Measures for the two questions | Repeat after failure, missed reply, forgotten intention, invalid output, by context size, pressure and fatigue; continuity by commitments, reuse, carried skills and forgetting | Behaviours that objective logs can show without judging text |
| EV-D6 | Probe design | Up to 20 key turns per run, 5 + 5 samples, modal choice compared, both labels | Small enough to afford, enough to see a clear change |
| EV-D7 | M15 minimum occurrences | 3 content-checked chains of 2+ hops; 2 learned rules with 1 via watching or telling, plus at least 1 learned grass-cord axe across the three on-arm runs, traced from an island event to the axe decision; a per-seed report (met, tried, succeeded, learned) with no per-seed minimum; 3 non-maker uses with 1 on a later day; 3 judged cross-sleep agreements with 2 kept (owner, 2026-11-18) | The axe is the strongest sign of learning on the island; three seeds are too few to require it in two of them |
| EV-D8 | Artifact limits | 8 MiB per shard, 32 MiB index, split never truncate | Fits a browser load; long runs shard instead of failing silently |
| EV-D9 | Exploration registration | A reproducibility manifest only, no budget approval | P3.5 has no trial caps (ADR 0012 D4) |
