## Handoff

### What changed

- Added the complete engine §2 state model and the `initialize` / `World.step`
  boundary. The core projects RR-01 packs and WAP turns into typed engine
  input, binds turn identity and ordinals, advances the clock, and commits
  state and ordered events together.
- Added deterministic entity allocation, completion shuffle, kernel
  preconditions and actions, movement, item choice, transfers, split/merge
  lineage, and the world and receiving-tile limit checks. The event registry
  covers engine §11 and WAP §9.
- Connected signature-only hooks in `settle.py`, `processes.py`,
  `physiology.py`, `perception.py`, and `speech.py`. The hooks give
  EN-02 through EN-06 their phase boundaries and state. EN-07 owns durable
  snapshots and replay.
- Adopted CT-01's `world.toml` and `layout.map`. Core tests decode them into
  typed `WorldSetup` and use a synthetic extension of `engine-test` for
  kernel timings and limits. Initial sight, receipts, and delivery production
  use the EN-05 seam. The tests seed exposure at that seam.
