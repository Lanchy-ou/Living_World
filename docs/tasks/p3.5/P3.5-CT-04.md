### Deliverables

- Reuse the public world setup. After PR-07 merges, use its per-case completion receipts and campaign resume mechanism. Extend those receipts with references to M13 checkpoints, evidence and timing where needed. Keep a single completed-case index. Support a script consuming resident packets or stand-alone policy sessions with a frozen config and seed. Expose a per-boundary observer for metrics, a recorded-turn replay and an explicit run-or-resume call for downstream commands. Do not feed observer state into a decision.
- Pin revision/runner, policy or script and setup identity, arm, seed, config/map blob and effective hashes, vocabulary, pack ancestry, library, declared horizon and timing settings before work. Write a run ID, case index, attempt history and durable checkpoint location. Save the script program position, per-resident pending work or `PolicySession.checkpoint()`, observer progress needed to resume, and committed witness prefix along with the world checkpoint. Use `PacketJournal` and `save_checkpoint/load_checkpoint`. Record the exact last recoverable boundary. Preserve pending acknowledgements, RNG and next decision.
- Record cumulative active wall time from setup until final durable outputs, including I/O and attempted work across resumes, and explicit downtime separately. For budgeted cases persist timing progress frequently enough to account conservatively for interruptions. A missing bound on elapsed time leaves the timing verdict indeterminate. Include phase ticks, throughput, final hash and zero provider calls. Keep elapsed/host data outside deterministic trace comparisons.
- Publish each case completion only after outputs and hashes verify. A crash after a boundary or before a completion marker, or retry of an already complete case, must leave a single logical result. The parent run skips verified finished cases and resumes the unfinished one at its last good checkpoint.

Load the persisted world and sim state in a fresh session. Compare subsequent choices, journal packets, objective events, final state and reporter totals byte-for-byte where canonical.
- Stop again after the final boundary but before the case receipt. Recover the completion exactly once. Alter a pinned input or committed journal byte and show refusal before appending or counting anything. Retain the failed attempt. Exercise a time limit on a short controlled case without wall-clock-sensitive tests.
- Hand off the callable interface, example command from a downstream smoke fixture, sample receipt and resume command. Prove progress past an interior checkpoint. A restart of the full case under a new ID does not satisfy this card.
