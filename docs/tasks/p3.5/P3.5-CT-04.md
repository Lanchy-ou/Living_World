### What changed

Use `runtime.run_or_resume(directory, plan, setup)` for a single case. For a campaign, pass ordered `CampaignCase(plan, setup)` entries to `runtime.run_campaign(directory, cases)`. Each case has a unique index. A partial result returns the exact recoverable tick and checkpoint. The setup factory supplies the initialized world, blob digests, controller factory and observer. The smoke fixture uses PR-07’s public `setup_world` and frozen base policy.

The runner persists the EN-07 world checkpoint, pending delivery, script position or per-resident policy state, observer totals and the committed witness prefix. Each observed tick has an immutable packet batch and a recording of its turns and objective events. `runtime.replay_case(case_directory, pack)` replays the verified prefix. Campaign work directories are `work/<case_index>/`.

PR-07’s `bind_evidence` pins the campaign scope in `scope.json`. Each `inputs/<index>.json.gz` archives the M13 case descriptor, including the seed, controller, observer, setup, revision, runner, arm, content ancestry, source and effective hashes, opening snapshot, horizon and timing settings. PR-07’s `projection_sha256` binds these archived bytes. On a new case, M13 runs the scenario through its final checkpoint, verifies the outputs and publishes its payload to PR-07’s `cases/<index>.json` using `report_bytes` and `store_once`. On every handoff, including reopening a completed case, `run_recorded` verifies the receipt. M13 also verifies the checkpoint history, packet/turn prefix, final evidence digest and evidence/timing references before returning the result. The receipts under `cases/` are the sole campaign completion index.

Timing starts before setup. Successful execution includes recovery, attempted work, journaling, checkpoint I/O and verification through the durable completion receipt. Known downtime is measured separately. A budgeted case with an unmeasured interval after process death has an indeterminate verdict. Reopening a verified completion preserves its measured time and attempt count. Refusals record a failed attempt.
