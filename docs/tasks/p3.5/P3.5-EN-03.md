## Handoff

### What changed

The engine compiles schedules from validated rules at initialization and
when new rules enter the library. Phase 4a uses rule and subject ID order.
Phase 4b snapshots open process IDs at the end of 4a and tries the highest
priority eligible completion per process. In-reach contributors complete on
commit, with progress and output provenance recorded by process ID.

World rules, process completion, and `keep_progress` use the same physical
ledger as verb settlement. A refused branch preserves work, contents,
allocator state, and effect events. Contents binding preserves the first
lexicographic allocation and prunes infeasible prefixes.

The EN-30 selected-branch check uses the engine-test analogue
`test_rr37_chance_branch_selected_before_spill_admission`.

Edited shared engine files: `effects.py` (shared ledger and world operands),
`settle.py` (interruption and input consumption), and `tick.py` (schedule
lifecycle and phase hooks).
