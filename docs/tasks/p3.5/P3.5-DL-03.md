**Deliverables**

- Implement deterministic recipe dispatch by reason and a public registration hook. Honor commitment priority, exclusions, eligibility, aggregate limit, tie-break and per-period deduplication. Persist each returned memory's revision and rendered hash in typed recall entries.
- Implement the one-query decision → recall → decision_after_recall sequence. Use only the reachable index; materialize accepted IDs once. Rebuild the act request with this turn's recall entries protected at the floor. The off arm appends the local off_empty entry and makes the second decision without a recall call. A second recall is an invalid turn.
- Validate citations against the sent manifest of the adopted act only. Bind the immutable rendered fragment/revision to the act call. Provide typed local entries for recipe, deliberate and off_empty; keep invalid citations for inspection. Credit a record through the memory store once on its first actual decision-stage dispatch for a turn.

**Acceptance (positive, negative, edge, determinism)**

- DL-09, DL-22 — Apply the §6.1 strength formula and §6.2 recipes (threshold 20, strongest first then lowest ID, total ≤5). Exclude history, refuted skills and previously recalled/prefix records. Open commitments with a silent visible counterpart or due within 24 ticks come first regardless of strength. Filter a fallback action end before selecting any outcome recipe.
- DL-10 — Deliberate recall accepts at most five eligible IDs of strength ≥40, records ignored IDs and ends with an act; a repeated recall is a shape error. Check both the three-call on path and the off-empty two-decision path.
- DL-21, DL-28, DL-30 — Kept entries and the rendered current intention ID in both arms can be cited. Unknown, trimmed, full-index-only, later revisions and other requests' fragments cannot stand in for the sent one. Hide raw acceptance IDs. Deduplicate use across retries/follow-up calls for a turn; credit after dispatch even if the provider failed. Omitted and never-dispatched records, scanned index rows and rendering the current intention alone get zero.
