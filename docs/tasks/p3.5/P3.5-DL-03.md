## Handoff

### What changed

`RecipeCues.from_delivery` takes DL-02's filtered delivery, the scheduled reasons and notification classes, and the IDs of submitted `act` intents. A neutral fallback end report supplies no outcome cue. `RecipeRegistry.register(reason, predicate, *, kind=None)` is the extension hook for MM-02. Its predicates receive the turn's cues and one resident projection. The registry applies the same strength threshold, ranking, five-record limit and period exclusions to their matches.

`run_recall_turn` prepares each decision stage with DL-02 and passes the admitted request and its journal to the host's retrying caller. Deliberate recall pins its eligible index after the first decision dispatch. The first five response slots can select each indexed ID once. The receipt retains ignored IDs in model order. The off arm records a local empty entry and goes directly to the follow-up decision. A guard stop or failed call returns its stage evidence to DL-04.

The host uses `store_credit(store)` for decision dispatches and calls `bind_adopted_act` with the adopted reply and its day entries. Recall receipts identify the producing turn or call, memory revision, entry revision and rendered bytes. Citation receipts bind the valid IDs to the adopted attempt, wire hash and matching sent fragments. Use receipts identify the first exposing call and store-version change for each credited revision.
