A blocking finding must be resolved before the affected P3.5 specifications are frozen. Non-blocking findings should be resolved while making those specifications and task cards concrete. ### F-01: concrete counterexample and closure conditions

At the end of one day, a memory-off resident writes the intention “return the axe to B tomorrow at the northern cave.” After waking, the harness leaves the morning long-term selection empty and returns nothing from recall. It can still inject the old intention through the current-state section, exactly as ADR 0013 D4/D6 otherwise requires. Those three ablation switches alone therefore do not establish the intended condition. Similarly, removing consolidation removes the normal context-close path unless the off arm has its own recorded lifecycle transition.

There is also an inherited source outside the long-term store. The frozen interface contract exposes `explored_static_tiles`, and the experience specification expressly leaves this cumulative knowledge in the latest world-owned perception. It is historical terrain knowledge, not a hidden dynamic stock query, but it still comes from earlier exploration. The review does **not** assume that v2 must retain that exact field. The missing decision is how the new no-prior-period-information condition handles the inherited behavior when the v2 perception and evaluation specs are frozen.

Resolve this as one boundary, rather than a list of unrelated feature switches:

1. Define the off arm's transition to a new accessible working period, independently of whether a consolidation call runs. Reconcile that transition with the chosen nap semantics.
2. Start that period without earlier cognitive content in the day log, current intention, dialogue state, prefix or recall. Keep the resident's identity, base personality and current physical observations.
3. Define which incoming perception information is current observation and which is retained history. Filter the latter on the agent side as required by the accepted ablation, while preserving world-owned exposure receipts and physical state.
4. Retain objective events and mechanical records for analysis, without opening a resident-facing route back to them. Record the reset/projection so restored and recorded runs apply the same boundary.
5. Verify the boundary with a small deterministic scenario containing a unique prior-period intention, an unfinished dialogue and previously explored terrain now outside sight. The next context must contain no carried-over cognitive fact from those sources. Current inventory, position, visible terrain and physical consequences must remain available.

The blocking basis is the unaddressed interaction with inherited frozen-spec behavior and the conflicting context paths. It is not a claim that the two-arm experiment is infeasible or that the owner should reverse the accepted memory-off decision.

### 8.4 Focused assessment of context pressure

#### 8.4.1 World isolation

The allowed path is `world delivery -> agent assembly -> derived pressure -> model proposal -> ordinary world validation`. There is no reverse pressure-state channel. The engine may receive a resident's validated sleep intent; it must not receive pressure as a new precondition, physiology input or forced-sleep trigger. This follows 0013 D1/D4 and 0012 D3, not an exception to them.

Keep the typed pressure value, numerator/denominator, band and rendering identity outside `Perception`, `WorldState`, world rules and the objective event catalogue. Store them in agent decision/context records and project them read-only into inspection. World mental fatigue continues to use world quantities only. Neither emitting a pressure cue nor shortening its rendering may count as another world experience delivered to the resident.

“Never reaches the world” means no authoritative pressure telemetry or rule dependency. It cannot sensibly prohibit a resident from voluntarily saying “my thoughts feel crowded”: the world may record that utterance as speech without treating it as measured fatigue or pressure. Likewise, free-text action reasons are not physical authority. Do not censor speech or parse its pressure claims into world state to enforce the boundary.

A suitable isolation fixture holds world inputs and recorded intents constant while changing only the pressure profile: perception bytes, physical transitions and factual events must remain unchanged. A separate model experiment may produce different intents; those indirect behavioral differences are intended, not a boundary violation.

#### 8.4.2 Exact requests, counts and replay

Exact reconstruction is feasible, but a pure counter is necessary rather than sufficient. [P3 section 2.2][r3-counter] requires pinned pure counting of the complete charged input, including schema framing, or a proven upper bound; it forbids network counting. [Section 7.3][r3-replay] additionally forbids invoking the provider counter in recorded execution. Preserve the P3 path unchanged and specify the P3.5 path explicitly. A local, versioned derivation helper is different from calling a provider adapter during replay.

The specification needs the following contracts; none requires choosing a numerical threshold in this review:

1. **Measurement domain.** State whether pressure measures the retained waking-period representation or the proposed model-visible request. Enumerate fixed instructions, identity/personality, morning memories, day-log entries and recall, current perception/state, explored-map rendering, framing and the one structured-output/tool schema. Distinguish excluded audit/pending records. Section subtotals must use a declared attribution method: independently tokenizing fragments is not automatically the count of their concatenation.
2. **Self-reference and ordering.** State whether the pressure field's name, value and prose count toward the pressure calculation. Do not recursively count the final request to choose the sentence that defines that same final request. Freeze ordering among ingestion, recall, cue derivation, guard projection and request hashing. Retry the same frozen request unless a separately recorded policy permits otherwise.
3. **Capacity semantics.** Pin the effective provider/model window and whether it is an input-only limit or a shared input/output capacity. Account for the applicable output/reasoning allowance and protocol overhead. Warning thresholds are separate from the hard admission limit and must leave room for the cue and a valid action response. No bare ratio to a model marketing label proves fit.
4. **Identity and purity.** Retain counter algorithm/version/hash, tokenizer assets when used, schema and renderer versions, pressure mapping/wording, window/settings and any guard profile. Define deterministic integer comparisons and equality cases. No live counting endpoint, mutable “latest” tokenizer, cache-discount adjustment or unrecorded calibration from returned usage may choose a cue.
5. **Derived provenance.** Record the input-context manifest/hash, counted amount and domain, band, cue bytes or immutable rendering reference, and guard outcome in agent records. Pressure is not an `observed` world fact. Request hashing must bind the cue and the final projection; a changed counter or missing input must refuse verification rather than silently select another band.
6. **Replay.** Rebuild from retained parts and recorded model-selected memory results. Recompute the pure derivation using the pinned implementation or verify a recorded derived result under a specified contract; never ask a model or provider to recreate it. Distinguish exact reconstruction of retained requests from the separate question of how accurately a local estimator matches hosted tokenization.
7. **All call types.** Apply window admission to morning selection, deliberate recall and both consolidation calls, not just action decisions. A whole day that fits a decision request may not fit together with a memory index, selected old records, pending days and a change-set schema. The pending-area limit alone is not a proof that the consolidation request fits.

One possible acyclic construction, offered as a specification option rather than a new Owner decision, is:

```text
R0 = pinned rendering of eligible stored parts, excluding the pressure field
n0 = pinned local count or certified bound for the declared R0 domain
p  = pinned band mapping(n0, pinned capacity definition)
R1 = render the same parts with the exact cue for p
n1 = count/bound the complete final provider input, including the cue and schema
admit R1 only if it fits the applicable input/output envelope
otherwise use the separately specified, recorded guard path
```

Here the cue does not count itself in `n0`, but it does count in admission `n1`. Other constructions can work, including a fixed reserved cue allowance, provided their semantics are explicit and bounded. Do not silently recalculate the band from a shorter degraded request and thereby label a discarded day as cognitively fresh; record raw pressure and delivered size separately where they differ.

DeepSeek provides an offline tokenizer but directs users to returned usage for processed counts; Anthropic explicitly describes even its server count as an estimate and documents system-added tokens. These are reasons to distinguish reproducibility, admission safety and billed usage, not permission to introduce a network counter into this project. See [S5][r3-s5] and [S6][r3-s6].

#### 8.4.3 Description, autonomy and the overflow guard

The current direction is descriptive, but the sample sentence is not a complete acceptance oracle. Render the fatigue clause from the actual delivered fatigue band and the pressure clause from its own agent value. High pressure with low world fatigue must not automatically say “you are very tired.” A description such as “your thoughts feel crowded” differs from “stop now,” “choose sleep,” or “sleep for K ticks.” The latter statements prescribe an action or disclose the intentionally unannounced consolidation duration. The resident must remain able to continue, speak, choose another action or decline sleep; muting and hard-stop semantics do not change.

Pressure-based choice still influences behavior—that is its purpose. “Does not decide for the resident” means no action override or deterministic restriction, not that the prompt has no influence. The cue must not create a hidden compulsory turn, overwrite an intention, or convert ordinary rest into sleep. Resting or a short nap need not close the cognitive period.

An exceptional guard need not violate the ban on **hidden** compression, but disclosure is necessary and not sufficient. A conforming degradation path must preserve the canonical original day log; state exactly what model-visible entries were omitted or transformed; record pre/post manifests, hashes and policy identity; and expose the degraded condition to inspection and, where information is missing, to the resident without inventing replacement facts. It must not change world fatigue, force sleep, fabricate a completed consolidation or let the off arm retrieve closed/pending history. Evaluation must identify affected turns rather than attribute behavior after truncation to natural long-context performance.

A logged daytime LLM summary is not automatically authorized: reconcile it explicitly with 0012 D2's consolidation-only memory-formation rule and 0013's verbatim adopted-response rule. Keeping originals does not by itself make a new interpretation harmless. Deterministic projection/omission is a different operation. Likewise, local overflow is not automatically a provider failure under 0013 D7; silently invoking the rule policy would widen an accepted fallback trigger. Any such widening needs an explicit decision. A controlled suspension can avoid corruption but is not proof that an acceptance run completed.

The final strategy remains open. The specification must show how it prevents oversized dispatch even when the cue is ignored or a single new input crosses the reserve in one step. It must not rely on the model agreeing to sleep. MemGPT's warning is backed by queue management, and Letta's documented compaction changes the input representation; neither establishes that a warning alone is sufficient here. [S1][r3-s1], [S2][r3-s2].

#### 8.4.4 Evaluation, arm differences and naps

**The arms can start at different pressure.** With equal renderer, window and counter policy, morning long-term memories can give the on arm a larger initial numerator. Extra pressure may induce earlier sleep, changing exploration time and consolidation frequency. This is a mediated effect of the whole memory system, consistent with 0014 D2's stated comparison; it is not evidence isolating memory-content quality or consolidation quality. Report prefix-memory tokens, shared overhead, waking baseline pressure, within-period growth, turn counts, sleep timing and guard events separately. Do not secretly subtract on-arm memories or pad the off arm merely to obtain equal starting pressure.

**Short naps should not reset pressure by lifecycle fiat.** A sleep shorter than K retains the same period's prefix and day log and does not rerun morning selection. World fatigue may improve while retained-context load remains. That agrees with “a nap does not clear the mind.” It does not require the numerical count to be exactly constant: current-state rendering can change, and new delivered observations can add text. A completed period may reduce the load, but the new prefix means pressure is not necessarily zero.

**Separate measurement is possible; causal separation is not supplied by logging alone.** Keep fatigue, pressure and exact cue text as distinct fields, with their sources. Correlation across a day cannot establish which cue caused sleep. The Q5 probes can vary one cue on copies of a recorded context, with pinned other inputs and repeated samples. Such probes test prompt interventions, not a change to physical fatigue. Report residual length/position effects and uncertainty. A short nap provides useful discordant observations, but is not a randomized intervention.

For memory-removal probes, resolve R3-F-02: holding pressure fixed asks a different question from rebuilding the complete counter/cue/guard pipeline. Neither probe modifies the source run or its replay. Long-context findings also depend on evidence position and distractors, not just occupancy; the existing pressure value is not a substitute for behavioral evaluation. [S3][r3-s3], [S4][r3-s4].
