- Date: 2026-11-17.

- Review role: independent Gate 2 design review. The reviewers did not author the reviewed ADRs or plan.
- Scope: design consistency, principle boundaries, acknowledged supersession, missing architectural decisions, testability and milestone dependencies. This is not implementation acceptance or a live-run result.

## 1. Assessment and severity

The architecture preserves the essential separation between world settlement, resident decisions, subjective memory and developer-only analysis. The protocol/vocabulary/rule split, perceived rather than hidden rule feedback, recorded consolidation changes, and archived v1 equivalence fixtures provide a coherent basis for the next phase. The major intentional v1 departures—action queues, soft interrupts, speech, ownership restrictions and trial caps—are acknowledged in the relevant ADRs.

There is **one blocking finding**: the accepted memory-off condition does not yet have a complete boundary across context lifecycle, separately injected cognitive state and inherited historical perception. Three further findings clarify document or gate semantics; they do not require changing the architecture.

A blocking finding must be resolved before the affected P3.5 specifications are frozen. Non-blocking findings should be resolved while making those specifications and task cards concrete. Comments on owner-approved directions are in a separate table and do not affect the verdict. Existing open questions are not counted again as newly discovered defects.

### F-01: concrete counterexample and closure conditions

At the end of one day, a memory-off resident writes the intention “return the axe to B tomorrow at the northern cave.” After waking, the harness leaves the morning long-term selection empty and returns nothing from recall. It can still inject the old intention through the current-state section, exactly as ADR 0013 D4/D6 otherwise requires. Those three ablation switches alone therefore do not establish the intended condition. Similarly, removing consolidation removes the normal context-close path unless the off arm has its own recorded lifecycle transition.

There is also an inherited source outside the long-term store. The frozen interface contract exposes `explored_static_tiles`, and the experience specification expressly leaves this cumulative knowledge in the latest world-owned perception. It is historical terrain knowledge, not a hidden dynamic stock query, but it still comes from earlier exploration. The review does **not** assume that v2 must retain that exact field. The missing decision is how the new no-prior-period-information condition handles the inherited behavior when the v2 perception and evaluation specs are frozen.

Resolve this as one boundary, rather than a list of unrelated feature switches:

1. Define the off arm's transition to a new accessible working period, independently of whether a consolidation call runs. Reconcile that transition with the chosen nap semantics.
2. Start that period without earlier cognitive content in the day log, current intention, dialogue state, prefix or recall. Keep the resident's identity, base personality and current physical observations.
3. Define which incoming perception information is current observation and which is retained history. Filter the latter on the agent side as required by the accepted ablation, while preserving world-owned exposure receipts and physical state.
4. Retain objective events and mechanical records for analysis, without opening a resident-facing route back to them. Record the reset/projection so restored and recorded runs apply the same boundary.
5. Verify the boundary with a small deterministic scenario containing a unique prior-period intention, an unfinished dialogue and previously explored terrain now outside sight. The next context must contain no carried-over cognitive fact from those sources. Current inventory, position, visible terrain and physical consequences must remain available.

The blocking basis is the unaddressed interaction with inherited frozen-spec behavior and the conflicting context paths. It is not a claim that the two-arm experiment is infeasible or that the owner should reverse the accepted memory-off decision.

## 3. Comments on owner-approved directions

| C-01 | [0007 D10][protocol-watching]; [0011 D3][memory-skills]; [0014 D2][eval-learning] | Learning by watching is supported, but the first-success taxonomy only counts own failures, spoken teaching and earlier-day memory as island learning. A resident can watch another resident's attempts, then succeed on its first attempt before sleeping or hearing an explanation. That supported path may be labelled possible prior knowledge. | Add a witnessed-attempt attribution path, linked to the watcher's own delivered perception and visible phenomena. | C-02 | [0014 D2][eval-capabilities]; [0011 D3][memory-skills] and [D7][memory-carriers]; [0012 D1–D2][formation-writes] | Some metrics depend on told memories and skill hypotheses formed at consolidation. Report long-term-store quality separately, or mark that representation-specific measure not applicable in the off arm. |
| C-03 | [0014 D2][eval-ablation]; [0012 D1–D2][formation-writes] | Memory-on versus memory-off changes access to mechanically recorded commitments, transfers and evidence as well as model-formed memories. A difference estimates the effect of the whole cross-period memory system; it does not isolate the consolidation model's contribution. | Keep the accepted two arms and qualify the conclusion accordingly. | C-04 | [0011 D3][memory-skills]; [0007 D4][protocol-perception]; [0010 D2][starter-content] | Skill scoring maps perceived descriptors to true property ranges, but deceptive appearance is a deliberate mechanic. A global equation between “looks strong” and a true strength band would mis-score the very learning case intended to challenge prior knowledge. | Score hypothesis predictions against actual material/instance outcomes, or explicitly use the material/instance-specific apparent-to-true mapping in analysis. Keep hidden values out of resident memory. |
| C-05 | [plan §4][plan-milestones] and [§10][plan-deferred]; [0013 D10][loop-tracing]; [P3 closeout][p3-closeout] | The replacement long rule-based run demonstrates world performance, but need not exercise growing contexts, pending consolidation, content-addressed call records and multi-resident inspection artifacts at the M15 scale. The deferred P3 proof remains deferred; the new data path still deserves its own evidence. | Before the full live matrix, consider a recorded/fake multi-resident cognitive workload over the intended horizon, without provider calls. Treat it as a P3.5 workload, not a silent restart or relabelling of the old P3 proof. |
| C-06 | [plan §4, M14][plan-milestones]; [0010 D1–D2][starter-content] | M14 may use the compatibility world independently of M13, while its owner checks emphasize self-found skills on the deceptive starter rule. That rule is outside the compatibility pack. The basic memory loop can be demonstrated there, but the starter-specific evidence cannot. | When writing the task card, label starter-specific observations as later M15 evidence if M14 uses compatibility content, or make that demonstration depend on a small accepted starter slice. ## 4. Coverage and existing readiness work

| Area | Review conclusion |
|---|---|
| World authority and perception | The ADRs keep settlement and physical state in `world`; rule IDs and true properties stay outside resident-facing feedback. No additional confirmed world-authority or hidden-truth blocker was found. F-01 concerns historical knowledge in an experimental arm, not an assertion that current perception is omniscient. |
| Memory provenance | The model proposes changes; validation checks resident ownership, cited inputs, source kinds and versions. Wrong but sincerely held beliefs remain allowed. Source validation does not claim to prove the semantic truth of arbitrary natural language. |
| Replay and audit | Recorded changes, context manifests and inaccessible audit history are coherent directions. F-04 clarifies scheduling. This review does not claim that replay or restore has been implemented or executed for P3.5. |
| v1 migration | The separate compatibility pack, committed projection fixtures and archived v1 tag acknowledge the major behavioral changes. F-02 fixes the approval sequence. Exact projection/seed choices remain the declared open question. |
| Evaluation and dependencies | F-01 closes the off-arm boundary; F-03 assigns extension evidence. C-01–C-06 identify interpretation or sequencing improvements without reopening owner decisions. |
| Scope | Rule proposals remain a future extension constraint; weather, seasons and live viewing remain deferred. The P3 closeout and plan explicitly preserve the distinction between deferred work and passed validation. |

The current brief already marks **0012 D2 and 0013 D3/D4 as not ready for acceptance in their unfinished parts**. That is a declared readiness condition, not a new finding. Resolving the review findings does not silently close those discussions or complete M11: M11 still requires the owner's decisions, the approved protocol change request and eight written, frozen successor specifications.

No new findings are raised merely for the already listed open questions about K, retry/recovery, the context-window guard, capacities, vocabularies, commitment fields, rule priorities, conservation units, metric thresholds or probe sample counts. The provider/model window and rough cost in the brief are planning assumptions to be pinned and measured under the existing gates; this review supplies no provider verification or live cost evidence.
