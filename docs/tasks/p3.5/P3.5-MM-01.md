### What changed

The seven registered kinds share a strict envelope, ordered sources and a resident projection. The store allocates IDs per resident, applies an ordered batch atomically, and emits content-addressed bodies and versioned head changes. A snapshot resolves its own revision pins. Decision dispatches credit the prefix and recall revisions actually sent, once per record and turn. The resident view builds the index from the same projected records used for reads. Commitments expose their terms and hearing status. Social facts link to local commitment IDs.

Setting, clearing and resetting intentions preserves bounded history. The consolidation coordinator has a separate resident-local pending area with selective consumption and oldest-first eviction. Profile selection hashes the exact schema 0.2 file bytes and initializes the five bounded traits. The test fixtures `synthetic_min` and `synthetic_max` exercise both ends of the trait scale.
