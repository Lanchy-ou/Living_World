## Handoff

### What changed

All six call kinds have pinned prompts and output schemas. Decision schemas incorporate
the enabled world vocabulary. The Responses adapter uses the Flash-1017 counter and
retains the exact UTF-8 body sent to the byte transport. The offline preflight covers
the provider's recorded M9 regex rejection. Hosted schema acceptance belongs to DL-06.

The assembler supplies ordered parts, omission order, protected positions and the pressure
slot where applicable. The decision helper protects the latest 20 log entries and the
current deliberate recall, then removes prefix memories in reverse selection order.
Preparation stores full, measured candidates and floor by hash. Replay accepts resolved
parts from the coordinator, recomputes the full pressure cue and verifies the wire.

The coordinator injects two write operations into `Journal`. Bind `keep` to
`persistence.v2.journal.store_once` and `create` to `persistence._files.atomic_create`
under a per-run directory. The exclusive dispatch fact precedes transport I/O. A terminal
links the raw bytes, separate reasoning, typed result or error, latency and available
usage. Restore takes the host's existing facts and returns the recorded terminal or the
P3 interrupted disposition. The tests exercise these operations on disk with a fake clock
and scripted byte transport.
