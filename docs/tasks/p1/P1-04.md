### Deviations and decisions for review

- OQ-019 is implemented and tested with the rule facts' original actor and target IDs.
  OQ-020 and OQ-021 continue to use P1-03. Under the task card's OQ-022 default, the
  death interruption immediately follows that resident's death, corpse and ordered
  drop facts. Its only cause is that resident's `resident_died` event.
- OQ-040 needs an owner-approved restore contract change for full historical parity.
  A restored engine recognizes the initial event, the restored boundary's committed
  sequence, and the request of a saved reply that passed start validation. For other
  pre-checkpoint requests a new reply fails `INVALID_REPLY`. Older causal IDs require
  evidence from the restored snapshot or the current engine's event stream.
- OQ-041 through OQ-044 record the movement failure mapping, the tick-zero hash
  point, self-target and dead-attempt handling, and speech event linkage. Malformed
  speech acts are tested at the frozen DTO boundary per CR-001.
- OQ-045 and OQ-046 delimit the frozen output. Sim owns plan completion, first
  decisions, and deferred-trigger accumulation. World emits physical triggers and
  builds one internal terminal result with the interval's direct deltas and event IDs.
  The packet carries the filtered feedback. Earlier progress remains in objective facts.
