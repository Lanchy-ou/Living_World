### Hypothesis strategy summary

- **Content:** half the draws use the default content (period 144, offsets 0/24/48/72/96/120);
  the rest use a legal variant: `regrow_ticks` 2..16, six offsets each in `0..regrow_ticks-1`,
  `regrow_per_cycle` 1..3, `max_stock` 1..6 and `initial_stock` 0..`max_stock`. Small periods
  make every offset fire many times inside a run.
- **Start:** nine needs in `0..max`, health per resident from `1..600` or `1..10000` (so deaths
  occur), canteen servings 0..2; residents keep their spawn tiles.
- **Machine:** each rule advances 1..15 ticks (40 examples, 20 rules each). In every tick each
  living resident takes exactly one action drawn from the actions legal at that moment, so the
  activities are mutually exclusive by construction (`TickActivity` is built from that single
  action). Legality: `gather` only on a tree with stock from a Manhattan-adjacent tile with
  room; `fill` and `drink_spring` only next to the spring; `drink_canteen` only with servings;
  `give_*` only to a living neighbour with room; drops land on the actor's own tile and
  pick-ups happen only there. Destinations are drawn from tiles beside trees, the spring, the
  cave and a few other passable tiles; step counts 1..8; quantities from what is held or on
  the tile.
- **Phases per tick:** rest/sleep gain (phase 2), settlement (3), regrowth (4), metabolism and
  deaths (5), then the invariants: integer bounds, stock within the cap, config-based fruit
  conservation, canteen in one place with a living holder and room, dead residents hold
  nothing, one corpse per dead resident, unique ground-pile tiles and handles, slot limits.
- **Late regrowth phases:** besides the variants, a 300-tick random legal run on default
  content asserts every regrowth falls on `k % 144 == offset` and each tree's first regrowth is
  at `offset or 144`; a deterministic rotating-policy run asserts ticks 72, 96, 120 and 144 all
  fire. Hypothesis event statistics show `fill`, `drink_canteen`, gives, drops and pick-ups
  each occur; `fill` is rare in the machine, so a scripted legal run covers it directly.
- **Synthetic stress** (illegal sequences, arithmetic invariants only) stays in
  `test_physical_rules_properties.py` under its own names.
