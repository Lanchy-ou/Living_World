## Handoff

### What changed

New private world modules (all pure integer logic, no IO, no RNG, no new dependencies):

- `world/space.py`: `StaticMap` (terrain, entry cost, opacity), clock and `sight_radius`,
  `line_points` / `has_line_of_sight` / `visible_tile_indices`.
- `world/pathfinding.py`: `find_path` returning an immutable `PathResult`.
- `world/perception.py`: descriptors (`need_band`, `fruit_band`, `visible_signs`,
  `own_condition`, `own_inventory`) and `sample_sight`.
- `world/receipts.py`: `merge_tile_indices`, `merge_receipts`, `receipt_authorizes_entity`,
  `receipt_authorizes_pickup`.
- `world/observation.py`: `register_content` and the three port bodies. A
  `WorkingAccumulator` subclass of the frozen `ObservationAccumulator` holds the private
  per-tick data.
- `world/ports.py`: the three port bodies now delegate to `observation.py` (function-level
  imports break the import cycle); no signature or model changed.
- `docs/spec/open-questions.md`: OQ-009 to OQ-013 appended.
- Tests: 91 new, in `test_space_los.py`, `test_pathfinding.py`, `test_perception_sampling.py`,
  `test_observation_ports.py`, plus the builder module `world_builders.py`.

Behaviour worth knowing for P1-04 and the reviewer:

- `observe_step` is called after each entered tile with the actor's position and consecutive
  1-based `step_index` (anything else raises `ValueError`). `finish_observation_tick` does the
  boundary scan for every living resident from the current `WorldState` and builds the batch.
- `physical_samples_by_actor` covers every living resident. `new_static_tiles_by_actor` and
  `exposure_receipt_updates` list only actors with something new. Applying a batch twice is
  harmless (the merge helpers are idempotent), and a repeated tick yields empty updates.
- The explored footprint in a sample is the existing footprint plus this tick's union, so it
  already includes tiles the engine has not yet written back.
- `completed_tick == 0` is the tick-zero scan: receipts are seeded and `new_sight_actor_ids`
  is empty.

### Findings

Paths beginning `world/` refer to `backend/src/living_island/world/`.
WR means World Rules and IC means Interface Contract.

- **G2-01 MAJOR — `world/pathfinding.py:68`, `:126`.** Search reads the unseen
  target's real passability and changes the result before any physical attempt.
  With explored indices `{0,1}` and target `(2,0)`, `",,W"` returns `ok`,
  a two-step route and cost 2. `",,^"` returns `PATH_BLOCKED`, a one-step
  route and cost 1. WR §5.3 requires the same tentative grass-cost route for
  either unseen target and defers passability and actual cost to step execution.
  Keep the unexplored final coordinate in the route and let P1-04 produce
  `PATH_BLOCKED` on attempted entry. Keep a pure blocked result for known
  impassable targets. The frontier predicate also treats an unlisted start as
  explored (`:48`); enforce adjacency to an already explored passable tile.
- **G2-02 MAJOR — `world/observation.py:76`, `:89`, `:243`.** Registration
  stores the caller's shallow-frozen `WorldContent` by reference. Changing its
  `need_band_cutoffs` list after registration changed Ella's band from
  `moderate` to `critical` with identical serialized `WorldState` and hashes.
  This violates verified content identity and deterministic replay (plan §2.1,
  IC §6.3). Bind a private immutable snapshot of verified content and test
  mutation of the supplied object. Resolve the binding design in OQ-009 before
  the engine uses these ports.
- **G2-03 MAJOR — `world/observation.py:135`, `:294`.** The public
  `receipt_baseline` accessor gives a defensive copy, but the novelty code reads
  separate mutable `known_ids`, `known_triples` and `known_cue_detail` indices.
  Adding `ryan` to `acc.known_ids["ella"]` suppressed Ella's `NEW_SIGHT`
  while the baseline receipt remained empty. Make the indices private and
  immutable, or read from an immutable baseline directly (plan §2.1, WR §5.1
  step 6).
- **G2-04 MAJOR — `world/perception.py:188`, `world/observation.py:264`.**
  A cue in sight appears only in `environmental_cues`. A step-only cue also
  never produces a transient entry. The probe exposed `moss_east_01` in both
  scenarios and got neither corresponding entity entry. WR §4.1 explicitly
  includes cues among visible entities, WR §5.1 step 5 covers intermediate
  entities, and IC §2.5 uses the same six entity types in both arrays. Emit
  ordinary cue entity sightings alongside the retained cue detail, or resolve
  OQ-010 through an approved change request before omitting them.
- **G2-05 MAJOR — `world/observation.py:284`, `:321`.** With radius zero,
  transferring the canteen from Ella to Mia at a completed boundary gave Mia
  her first canteen ID and receipt but no `NEW_SIGHT`. IC §2.1 counts own
  inventory as exposure and IC §2.5/WR §5.1 step 6 define novelty as first
  exposure of an entity ID, with self excluded. Compare newly exposed inventory
  IDs against the same tick-start baseline, retaining tick-zero suppression.
  If inventory is intended to be an exception, obtain an explicit owner decision.
- **G2-06 MAJOR — `world/perception.py:159`, `:167`,
  `world/observation.py:342`.** Content with a resident and a fruit tree both
  named `ryan` passes content validation (WR §1.1 specifies uniqueness in
  each domain). Seeing both fails the tick-zero scan with a duplicate
  `visible_entities` ID. IC §2.2 and §6.2 require stable ID-based references and
  ordering, and the current merge and novelty logic also assumes one identity
  per ID. Resolve the namespace or reserved-ID validation through plan §7 and
  add collision coverage. Silently dropping one entity would also lose exposure
  information.
- **G2-07 MINOR — `world/ports.py:3`.** The module docstring is changed outside
  the three allowed function bodies. Revert that hunk or record the scope
  exception. The frozen signatures and models are intact.
